# Store listing (itch.io page / app stores)

> The current card pool and art are for a **free, non-commercial fan build**
> (itch.io "No payments", direct APK). App stores need an original card pool
> and art first; reuse this text with the card names swapped.

## Name

Cards Clash

## Short description (80 characters)

Lane-based card battles: four lanes, five lands, 400+ cards. No ads, no purchases.

## Full description

Play creatures into four lanes, back them with buildings, cast spells and
**Floop** your creatures for powerful abilities. Every creature fights the
lane straight across; break through and hit the enemy Hero.

- **400+ cards** across Blue Plains, Corn Fields, Useless Swamp, Sandy Lands,
  Nice Lands and Rainbow, with realistic illustrations
- **69 Heroes**, each with a Hero Ability that recharges over a few turns
- **Campaign**: 8 regions, 80 battles, boss fights and 3-star goals
- **Modes**: Quick Play, Daily Dungeon, Gauntlet, Draft Arena, Sandbox
- **AI** from Easy to Nightmare, plus hot-seat play on one device
- **Online** (optional): Ranked seasons, Friendly rooms, cloud save
- Collection, deck builder with share codes, quests, chests and achievements
- 2D or 3D board, colour-blind landscape icons, text size and motion settings

No ads. No purchases. No tracking. Plays fully offline.

## Category / tags

Card game, strategy, collectible card game, turn-based, offline

## Content rating notes

Cartoon fantasy combat, no blood, no chat, no ads, no purchases, no user data
