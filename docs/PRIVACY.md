# Cards Clash — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

Cards Clash is a free fan game. It has **no ads, no purchases, no analytics and
no tracking**, and we **never sell or share your data**.

## Playing offline

Everything you do offline (collection, decks, progress, settings) is stored
only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Playing online (optional)

Online modes (cloud save, Ranked and Friendly matches) use a Supabase server.
Only if you choose to play online, we store:

| Data                                                                         | Why                                                       | Kept                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A random account id (guest) or your email address (if you link one)          | To sign you in and keep your progress across devices      | Until you ask us to delete it                                                                         |
| Your save (collection, decks, progress, currencies, profile name and avatar) | Cloud save                                                | Until you delete your account                                                                         |
| Match records and your rating                                                | To run matches, review cheating reports and show rankings | Finished matches: up to 30 days (the operator prunes them, see server/README.md); ratings: per season |

- Your email is used only to send sign-in codes. We never send marketing.
- Your profile name and rating are visible to other players.
- Data is stored by Supabase (supabase.com) on our project's servers and
  protected so that players can only read their own data.

## Children

The game has no chat, no ads and no purchases. Online play needs no personal
data (guest accounts are anonymous).

## Deleting your data

Signing out removes nothing from your device. To delete your online account
and everything stored with it, contact us at the address on the game's page;
we delete it within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with
a new date.
